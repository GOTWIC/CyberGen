# Nexus City (concept image)

## Prompt

> Nexus City from the concept image: keep its districts, water and transit layout, and add a sky
> tube loop linking the corporate core and the spaceport.

Image: `reference/nexus_city_concept.webp`

## Interpretation

- Read the image for layout hints only (skill section 4): north up, nine labeled districts,
  West Bay to the south west, Sentinel Canal as a wide channel entering from the east and opening
  into the bay between Old Town and Harbor Industrial, a tributary (named Kurosawa River after the
  hills) coming down the east side, Silver Island offshore to the south west.
- Districts and relations: Transit Nexus at the center as the anchor; Corporate Core north of it
  on the north edge; Aurora Spaceport north west on the coast; Neon Market west of the nexus and
  south of the spaceport; Northridge north east; Midtown east; Old Town south west across the
  channel; Harbor Industrial south east across the channel; Silver Island research campus on its
  own island.
- Transit from the legend: metro Blue Line around the corporate core; maglev Magenta Line from
  Neon Market through the nexus and Midtown to the east gate; the dashed elevated line as Harbor
  Line (spaceport to harbor over Horizon Bridge); plus the requested Spire Loop sky tube.
- Named places, landmarks and bridges kept from the labels (Sky Garden Plaza, Club Row,
  Nightfall Plaza, Central Plaza, Jade Canal, Temple District, South Docks, Freight Terminal,
  Kurosawa Hills, Riverside, East Gate, Baylink and Horizon bridges). Silver Span (to Silver
  Island) is new; the image shows a bridge there without a label.
- Scale: the image's scale bar implies about 14 km across; kept at 3.4 x 3.4 km (scope default
  range). `sea_share` lowered to 0.18 after the first schematic, because the default bay swallowed
  the land where Old Town belongs.
- Dropped: the "Arasaka" tower label (Cyberpunk 2077 IP). Kaze, Mirai and Helios kept as tower
  landmarks, the central spire became the Nexus Spire megatower.
