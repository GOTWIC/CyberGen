# 01 megablocks

Megabuildings in megablock districts rebuilt as a podium (4 to 7 floors) with a court through
it that opens to the sky, and separate towers on it (one to six, at least 16 m apart, at the
corners and along the edges) of stepped heights (58 to 90% of the tallest), each with a mid
setback and a crown; open decks join neighboring towers, and podium-level open decks join
neighboring podiums across streets. The height field is skewed harder (more low towers, a few
tall ones). Previews tint each building slightly differently. Greyhook and Nyxllor shown; all
44 checks pass on both.
