# WIP 01: local streets

Debug renders (not the final preview style) of the local street network of the five sample
cities: highways (amber), avenues (white, thick), streets (light gray), alleys (dark gray),
service roads (tan), pedestrian streets and waterfront promenades (green), footbridges
(magenta). Water is drawn roughly (river and canal shapes only partly filled).

- Patterns by archetype: rotated grids (corporate core), superblocks with U-shaped service
  lanes (megablocks), grid and park ring (midtown), organic growth steered by a cross field
  that bends along canals (old town, entertainment market), long-block grids and quay roads
  (port), loops with cul-de-sacs (residential heights), campus loops (research campus),
  radial and ring streets (transit hub), service rings around megasite reserves.
- Frame: frontage streets along highways (no street meets a highway), streets on district
  boundaries without a primary road, promenades 5 m from the water.
- Still open at this step: junction geometry, blocks, lots, open space, graphs, checks.
