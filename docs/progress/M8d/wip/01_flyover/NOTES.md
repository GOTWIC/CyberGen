# M8d WIP 01: first flyover trumpet

Vesper Bay's trumpet (ic02) with one loop and one flyover:
- the on loop starts at the stem's near terminal, passes under the deck along the stem and
  turns round its quadrant onto the far lanes;
- the flyover leaves the far lanes before the stem, wraps round the outside of the loop (the
  trumpet's bell), climbs to 16.6 m, crosses the deck square beside the stem and comes down
  beside the stem to a terminal on it.
