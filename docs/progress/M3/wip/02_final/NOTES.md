# WIP 02: final M3 builds

All five samples pass all 44 checks (13 new for M3). Since WIP 01:

- buildings face their main frontage, with entrances within reach of a walkway (door paths on
  huge lots);
- complexes move to a block that fits when their merged lots cannot hold them;
- Nexus City has three spires plus the megatower; Greyhook has the Lantern Wheel;
- megasites hold their parts (spaceport pads, hangars and fuel farm; freight berths, cranes
  and stacks; transit hall and platforms; stadium; parks; temples; skyports);
- the flight cone caps heights on a 45 degree surface;
- each massing close-up is centered on its district's buildings.
