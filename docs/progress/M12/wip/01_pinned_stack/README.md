# 01: the first stack in a city

Vesper Bay Pinned with the M12 art-direction change (two crosstown highways, the Stacks
Crosstown from the northeast exit to Vess Docks and the Bayline from the east exit to Neon
Strand, in place of the ring and spine). They cross in The Stacks at about 70 degrees: the
Stacks Crosstown is lifted to 9 m through the crossing, the Bayline stays at grade, and eight
directional ramps join them (tier 2 at 18 m, tier 3 at 27 m, right turns up to 9 m). All 77
checks pass on the first full-city try.

Later: the crosstowns proved fragile at the pinned demo's other sweep seeds, so the change
moved to a separate city, `cities/vesper_bay_stacks` (same spec otherwise, same seed, same
stack); the pinned demo stays as in M11.
