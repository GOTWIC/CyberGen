`preview_map.png`, `report.json` and `report.txt`; the four M0 cities also hold the spec they
were built from (`city_spec.json`, copied from `docs/progress/M0/`, two of them adjusted, see
below). Nyxllor is built from `cities/nyxllor/city_spec.json`. `city_model.json` is not

All checks pass for all five. Build times on the cloud container: 1.4 s to 12.5 s.

Spec changes for M1a (noted in each spec's `source.notes`):
- Nexus City: the coast opens to the `west` (was `south_west`, `sea_share` 0.18 -> 0.24) and the
  Sentinel Canal is pinned to reach the bay in the southwest (`to: {"edge": "bay", "at": 0.2}`).
  The concept shows open water along the whole west side with the spaceport on its northwest
  shore; a southwest corner bay left the northwest inland, so the spaceport's `coast: true`
  could not be met.
- Delta Megacity: `edge: south` dropped from the Industrial Port. The delta coast puts the river
  mouth on a lobe 1.2 km south of the center that a 1.8 km² port cannot sit on; `coast`,
  `south_of` and `along_river` already say "at the river mouth".
