# 01 shells

First shells of Vesper Bay (code at `ag1.5` to `ag1.7`): the whole city from far proxies on
the blockout's ground, two of the blockout's street cameras at full detail (the patchwork
street is from before the per-column patches), and two quick contact sheets (low-rise rows,
shanties and villas; towers, labs, warehouses and blocks), each building framed on its main
front. 18.8 M shell triangles for 5,793 buildings, built in 50 s with 4 workers; every shell
closed, every check passing.
