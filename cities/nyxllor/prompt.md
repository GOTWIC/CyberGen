# Nyxllor

## Prompt

Nyxllor: a coastal cyberpunk megacity on a bay that opens to the west. A wide river enters from
the east and splits into three delta channels before it reaches the bay, so most districts sit on
their own landmass. Corporate Core in the middle on a 45-degree grid, with a three-armed megatower,
a central plaza, twin towers and lots of skybridges. North of it, megablock residential stacks;
quiet residential heights in the northeast. A canal-laced Old Town of markets and temples on the
south bank. A neon entertainment strip on the waterfront between the core and the bay, lined with
palms. A container port and freight terminal on the bay in the southwest. A spaceport on an
offshore island in the bay, reached by a maglev causeway, and a research campus on an island in
the middle of the river. Transit: an elevated rail ring around the core, a north-south metro, a
maglev from the east exit through the core to the spaceport, and a sky-tube loop between the core
towers and the megablocks.

## Interpretation

- Bay on the west; Vess River from the east edge to the bay with 2 extra delta channels (three
  channels in all, per the skill's counting).
- Corporate Core anchors the center; every other district is placed relative to it.
- Launch Key (offshore, west) holds the spaceport; Scholar Isle (mid-river) holds the research campus.
- Palms along the strip expressed as greenery 0.4 on Neon Strand (palms are an M6 vegetation
  detail, not a spec field).
- Defaults kept on purpose: city seed, highway exits, bridge densities, heights outside the core.
